✦♡✦

01 / Your starting point

Enter your baseline information. Height is profile information; it is not a changeable fitness goal.

02 / Your personal goals

Set goals that are appropriate for your circumstances. These are user-entered targets, not health recommendations.

03 / Weekly FIT CHECK

Log a check-in and compare it with your previous entries. You can leave any measurement blank.

Measurements and weight are only one kind of information. Changes are not automatically good or bad; consider your wellbeing, strength, energy and professional health advice too.
